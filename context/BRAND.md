# BRAND DNA — KNIGHTDALE LAWN CARE

## Brand Mission
To connect homeowners in Knightdale, Wendell, and Zebulon, NC with dependable, vetted, independent local lawn care specialists.

## Brand Tone & Voice
- **Direct & Honest:** No high-pressure sales jargon, no exaggerated corporate claims.
- **Neighborly & Local:** Clear understanding of Eastern Wake County neighborhoods, weather patterns, and soil conditions.
- **Transparent:** Openly acknowledges its role as a referral service connecting homeowners directly with independent operators.

## Visual Design System
- **Primary Accent:** Deep Forest Greens (`#143823`, `#1b4332`, `#2d6a4f`) evoking healthy Carolina turf and evergreen trees.
- **Surface Neutrals:** Warm cream & crisp white backgrounds (`#fbfbfa`, `#ffffff`, `#f4f3ef`).
- **Call-to-Action Accent:** Rich Terracotta Amber (`#b45309`, `#d97706`) ensuring high contrast and immediate CTA legibility.
- **Typography:** Modern, clean system typography for fast performance and zero layout shift.
- **Imagery:** Authentic residential photography representing Piedmont North Carolina architecture, pine straw landscaping, and healthy lawn maintenance.
