# OFFER & CONVERSION STRATEGY — KNIGHTDALE LAWN CARE

## Primary Value Proposition
"Find dependable, independent lawn care specialists serving Knightdale, Wendell, and Zebulon without calling around or dealing with unresponsive contractors."

## Conversion Flow
1. **Free Quote Request:**
   - 60-second frictionless request.
   - Requires Name, Town, Service Type, Preferred Contact Method (Phone or Email), and Contact Detail.
   - No street address required up front.
   - Zero cost and zero obligation for homeowners.

2. **Lead Routing (Post-Launch):**
   - Dispatches via server-side webhook to selected local provider or CRM (e.g. Votel).
   - Provider follows up directly with customer to provide price quote and scheduling.

3. **Pre-Launch Guardrail:**
   - While provider onboarding is finalizing, the live form is disabled.
   - An honest pre-launch notice is displayed.
   - Design preview mode (`?preview=form`) allows testing validation without storing personal data.
